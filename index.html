🎯 Goal & Pedagogical Purpose

While browser-based electrophysiology dashboards such as EEGEdu (eegedu.com) allow students to inspect live waveforms, power spectra, and frequency bands through pre-built charts, a fundamental pedagogical bottleneck remains at the end of any guided curriculum: how can students, artists, and novice researchers transition from passively observing pre-configured charts to actively programming their own closed-loop neurofeedback environments, audio synthesizers, and cognitive neuroscience experiments?

Rooted in Seymour Papert's constructionism and the creative-coding pedagogy of Processing and p5.js, BrainImation provides a low floor (author a working closed-loop brain visualization in 11 lines of code with zero software installation), a high ceiling (online 4-channel ERP ensemble averaging, Modified Beer–Lambert Law fNIRS hemodynamics, Chowning FM audio synthesis, and 3D WebGL shaders), and wide walls spanning neuro-art, biofeedback gaming, sonification, and experimental psychology.

⚡ Core Engineering & Multimodal Signal Architecture

Traditionally, modulating a visual shape or sound with live EEG required stitching together an OS-specific Bluetooth daemon, Lab Streaming Layer (LSL) sockets, a Python/C++ DSP backend, and a separate rendering engine. BrainImation replaces that entire multi-process stack inside a single browser tab:

  • Two-Phase Lazy-GATT WebBluetooth Client (muse-browser.js): A zero-dependency 19.2 KB WebBluetooth driver (24.6× smaller than legacy muse-js) that resolves the lazy characteristic advertisement deadlock on modern Muse 2, Muse S, and Muse S Athena firmware while supporting both classic and Athena multi-wavelength optical presets (p21, p50, p1035, p1041).
  • 256 Hz 4-Channel EEG + Artifact-Rejected Hanning FFT: Streams microvolt time series across TP9, AF7, AF8, and TP10, screens 1.0 s (256-sample) windows for >150 μV EOG/EMG blink or muscle bursts, and computes 1/f-compensated relative band powers (delta, theta, alpha, beta, gamma) and composite attention and meditation indices.
  • 64 Hz Multi-Wavelength PPG / fNIRS & Cerebral Oxygenation: Streams Red (660 nm), Near-Infrared (730 nm), Infrared (850 nm), and Ambient optical channels to derive arterial pulse waves (eegData.ppgPulse), systolic Heart Rate (eegData.heartRate BPM), SpO₂ ratio-of-ratios, and Modified Beer–Lambert Law oxyhemoglobin ($\Delta[\text{HbO}_2]$), deoxyhemoglobin ($\Delta[\text{HHb}]$), and total hemoglobin ($\Delta[\text{HbT}]$).
  • 52 Hz 6-Axis IMU & Device Telemetry: Exposes 3-axis accelerometer and gyroscope kinematics, complementary-filtered head pitch and roll angles, and battery/temperature telemetry.
  • Hardware-Free Physiological Simulator (EEGSimulator): Synthesizes coupled 256 Hz 4-channel EEG (with realistic frontal blinks and N170/P300 evoked potentials), 64 Hz forward Beer–Lambert optical PPG waveforms, and 52 Hz IMU head motion so every sketch runs identically with or without a physical headset.

📊 Classroom Validation & Research Plan

Developed by Kyle E. Mathewson at the Department of Psychology and Neuroscience and Mental Health Institute, University of Alberta, BrainImation serves as the hands-on creative-coding and BCI studio following the guided EEGEdu curriculum.

  • Empirical Classroom Evaluation: Across $N = 69$ verified programs authored by $N = 30$ undergraduate learners in PSYCH 403, students expanded the concise 32-LOC starter scaffolds fivefold (median 155–164 LOC, $p < 10^{-11}$) to create custom closed-loop neurofeedback games, multi-band Web Audio synthesizers, and online 4-channel ERP oddball paradigms.
  • Unified Catalogue & Living Curriculum: BrainImation now unites over 90 interactive sketches across 14 categories—including progressive starter tutorials, stacked PPG/fNIRS hemodynamic derivations, 3D Muse S Athena / Muse 2 optode anatomy diagrams, multimodal Brain–Heart–Motion games, classic p5.eegedu sketches, and the 2022 NeuroArt Class Gallery.
  • Ongoing Roadmap: Expanding multimodal neurovascular coupling (EEG + fNIRS + HRV + kinematics), serverless URL & Data-URI portfolio sharing (?sketch=), and open-access classroom modules for reproducible neuroscience education.
